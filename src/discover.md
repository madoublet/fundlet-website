Fundlet works the same way for every merchant: organizations sell **your** products to their community, every order is tracked to a participant automatically, and the money runs through the checkout you already use. What changes is how a fundraiser fits your particular shop — which products travel well, when the season peaks, and what an organizer expects to see.

Pick the guide closest to what you sell.

---

## Sweets and treats

- **[Fundraising for Bakeries](/fundraising-for-bakeries)** — Baked goods fundraisers for schools and community groups, without the paper order forms.
- **[Fundraising for Candy Shops](/fundraising-for-candy-shops)** — Candy and confectionery drives with automatic per-participant tracking.
- **[Fundraising for Chocolate Shops](/fundraising-for-chocolate-shops)** — Chocolate fundraisers for chocolatiers and confectioners, run from your own store.
- **[Fundraising for Cookie Shops](/fundraising-for-cookie-shops)** — The classic cookie fundraiser, with the tallying handled for you.
- **[Fundraising for Popcorn Shops](/fundraising-for-popcorn-shops)** — Popcorn drives for schools, scouts, and teams, fully automated end to end.
