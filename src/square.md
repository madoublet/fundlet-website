Fundlet for Square turns your Square online store into a fundraising platform. Run a campaign, track who's selling what, and get paid through the Square account you already use.

The app, the tutorial library, and the setup guides all live on this page.

---

## Open the Square app

Sign in to manage your campaigns, participants, and payouts.

<p><a class="btn btn-primary" href="https://raise.fundlet.app" data-square-info>Open Fundlet for Square</a></p>

You'll be asked to log in to Square and approve access the first time you connect a store.
