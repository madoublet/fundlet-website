We want you to be completely satisfied with Fundlet. If you're not happy with your subscription for any reason, we're here to help.

### 30-Day Refund Guarantee

We will refund any payment made within the last 30 days, no questions asked, provided the payment has cleared Shopify's payment system and shows as refundable in your account. We do not refund any payment that is more than 30 days old. If you believe you are eligible for a refund, please contact us and we'll process it promptly.

### How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at [matt@matthewsmith.com](mailto:matt@matthewsmith.com) with your store name and the reason for your request. We'll respond within one business day.

### Contact Us

For questions about our refund policy, please reach out at [matt@matthewsmith.com](mailto:matt@matthewsmith.com).
