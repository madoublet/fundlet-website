Fundlet brings fundraising to your Square store without third-party platforms or complicated setup. Whether you're running a school fundraiser, supporting a local cause, or building a new revenue channel for your business, Fundlet lets you collect donations and track participant sales right from your own storefront.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Fundraiser

Setting up a fundraiser takes just a few minutes. From the Fundlet dashboard, you can create a campaign, set your goal, and customize the details to match your cause. Each campaign gets its own tracking page so participants and supporters always know where things stand.

Fundlet works with your existing Square product catalog — no need to build anything from scratch. Just connect your store, configure your campaign, and you're ready to go.

---

## Step 2: Onboard Your Participants

Once your fundraiser is live, you can invite participants — students, teammates, volunteers, or community members — directly through Fundlet. Each participant gets their own fundraising link so sales are attributed correctly and payouts are easy to calculate.

You can onboard participants one at a time or in bulk via CSV. Fundlet handles the tracking so you're not managing spreadsheets.

---

## Step 3: Sell Through Your Existing Store

Everything runs through your Square storefront. Supporters shop the way they normally would, and Fundlet connects each purchase to the right campaign and participant. There's no separate checkout, no third-party redirect, and no friction for your customers.

This also means your brand stays front and center throughout the entire buying experience.

---

## Who It's For

Fundlet is built for Square sellers who want a clean, professional fundraising experience:

- **Schools and PTAs** running annual fundraisers with product sales
- **Sports teams and clubs** raising money for equipment, travel, or uniforms
- **Nonprofits** looking for a storefront-based alternative to donation platforms
- **Small businesses** opening up a new community-driven revenue channel
