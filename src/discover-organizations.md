A Fundlet fundraiser works the same way for every organization: a shop you partner with lists **its** products on a campaign page in **your** name, each of your members shares a personal link, supporters check out online, and every sale is credited to the member who made it. No order forms, no cash envelopes, and no spreadsheet at the end of the drive.

What changes is how a campaign fits your particular group — how your costs are assessed, whose name the money follows, which deadlines you are raising against, and what your treasurer has to be able to prove.

Pick the guide closest to your organization.

## Sports and athletics

- **[Fundraising for Sports Teams](/fundraising-for-sports-teams)** — Uniforms, tournament travel, and equipment, with every sale credited to the player who made it.
- **[Fundraising for Youth Sports Leagues](/fundraising-for-youth-sports-leagues)** — One program across every team in the league, with per-team goals and per-player credit.
- **[Fundraising for Cheer and Dance Teams](/fundraising-for-cheer-and-dance-teams)** — Choreography, competition fees, and nationals travel, tracked per athlete.

## Are you a shop rather than an organization?

If you sell a physical product on Shopify or Square, you are the other half of this program — organizations sell your products to their community, and you gain customers who found you through a cause. Start with [Discover Fundlet for your Shop](/discover) for guides by what you sell, then [install Fundlet on Shopify](https://apps.shopify.com/fundlet) or [open it on Square](https://raise.fundlet.app).
