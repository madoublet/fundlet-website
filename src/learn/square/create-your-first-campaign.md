## Overview

Fundlet for Square was built for merchants already using the Square point-of-sale or Square Online system who want to add fundraising on top of it quickly. It's easy to use, and everything stays integrated with your existing Square setup — orders, checkout, and payments all run through Square.

This walkthrough covers logging in, setting up a campaign, and touring the fundraiser page your supporters see.

---

## Step 1: Log In and Connect Square

To get started, click **Add to Square Store** and hit **Continue to Square**. The first time you connect, you'll see an authorization prompt to link your Square account to Fundlet. After that, logging in is seamless.

---

## Step 2: Configure Your Shop Settings

When you land on the main page, you'll see your list of campaigns along with common shop-wide settings, including:

- **Branding** for your shop
- **Footer text**
- **Email settings** for the messages that go out

Set these up before you launch your first campaign so everything is consistent across your fundraisers.

---

## Step 3: Create a Campaign

Go to **Campaigns** and click **Add Campaign**. Once created, the main campaign interface gives you a clear overview:

- A list of **orders**
- A list of **participants**
- **Total sales**
- The **payout**, calculated from the settings you chose when creating the campaign

---

## Step 4: Add Participants

Open the **Participants** section to add the people selling on behalf of your fundraiser. You can add as many participants as you want, and upload a picture for each one. Every participant gets their own landing page, and you can email them directly using Fundlet's built-in email functionality.

---

## Step 5: Edit Campaign Details

Back in the campaign, click **Edit Campaign** to configure everything:

- **Products** — click **Add Product**, select the Square location, and pull your products in directly from Square
- **Dates** — set a beginning and end date for the campaign
- **Details** — add the title, name, description, and payout settings
- **Fulfillment** — enable shipping and/or allow pickup
- **Design** — upload a logo and hero image, and customize the campaign colors right from this page

---

## Step 6: Preview the Fundraiser Page

Click **View Landing Page** to see the finished result. The fundraiser page:

- Prompts supporters to select the participant they're supporting
- Shows a countdown of how much time is left in the campaign
- Displays a progress bar toward the fundraising goal
- Includes a share row to quickly spread the word
- Lists your products, which supporters add to their cart

---

## Step 7: Checkout Through Square

When a supporter is ready, they hit **Check Out** and are handed off to the **Square checkout**. Everything is integrated into your Square system — orders come through Square, the checkout is built with Square, and payments use your Square payment options.

That makes Fundlet a fast, well-integrated way to launch a fundraiser if you're already using Square point of sale or Square Online.
