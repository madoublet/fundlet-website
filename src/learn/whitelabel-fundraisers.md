## Overview

White labeling lets you remove all Fundlet branding so a campaign lives entirely on your own store. The URL no longer references Fundlet, there are no Fundlet discount codes, and emails come directly from you. Behind the scenes you are still using Fundlet, but to your shoppers and participants the experience is completely transparent — it looks exactly like something you built in-house.

White label features are available on **Pro plans and above**.

There are four pieces you can configure:

- Remove discount codes
- Remove "powered by Fundlet" from emails
- Change the fundraiser URL handle
- Send emails from your own custom domain

---

## Remove Discount Codes

You can now remove discount codes from your fundraisers entirely. Instead of using a discount code to track orders, Fundlet uses a metafield in the backend to attribute each order to the correct campaign and participant.

This tracking is completely transparent to the shopper — they never see a Fundlet discount code applied at checkout.

---

## Remove "Powered by Fundlet" from Emails

Turn off the "powered by Fundlet" branding that normally appears in fundraiser emails. Once removed, the emails your participants and shoppers receive carry only your branding.

---

## Change the URL Handle

By default, fundraiser pages live at a URL that references Fundlet (for example, `pages/fund/[campaign-id]`). You can now change the handle to something generic that fits your brand — for example, `pages/fundraisers`.

After you update the handle, fundraiser pages run on your own domain with no Fundlet branding in the URL.

> **Important:** Changing the URL handle affects **all** fundraisers — both current campaigns and any created going forward. If you change the handle on an existing fundraiser, set up a redirect from the old URL or re-communicate the new URL to anyone who already has the old link. Plan ahead for this before making the change.

---

## Send Emails from Your Custom Domain

This piece takes a little more setup. Rather than fundraiser emails coming from the `funlet.app` domain, you can configure them to send from your own domain, so every email your supporters receive comes directly from you.

Because this requires domain configuration, plan for a short setup process. Reach out if you need help getting it connected.

---

## Save and Verify

After configuring your white label settings, click **Save**. To confirm everything is working from the shopper's perspective:

1. Open a campaign and view the campaign page.
2. Add an item to the cart — the URL now reflects your custom handle (for example, `pages/fundraisers`) rather than referencing Fundlet.
3. Proceed to checkout — there is no longer a Fundlet discount code applied.

With these settings in place, your fundraiser runs natively in your store, on your domain, with your brand — start to finish.
