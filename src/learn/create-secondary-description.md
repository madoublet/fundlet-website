## Overview

Fundlet lets you add a secondary line of text to any product in your campaign — great for calling attention to specific items, surfacing dietary information, or highlighting a discount. The text appears in the product list beneath the product name and can be styled from the theme customizer.

---

## Step 1: Add Callout Text to a Product

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Products** and open the product you want to update.
2. Scroll down to **Metafields** and click **View All**.
3. Find the **Fundlet Callout Text** field and enter your secondary description (e.g. "Gluten-Free" or "Fall Favorite").
4. Hit **Save**.

Refresh your campaign page and the callout text will appear in the product list beneath the product name.

---

## Step 2: Style the Callout Text (Optional)

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store** and click **Edit** on your theme.
2. Navigate to your Fundlet campaign template.
3. Under the **Collection** section, find the **Callout Text** setting.
4. Choose a color to make the text stand out (e.g. orange).
5. Hit **Save**.
