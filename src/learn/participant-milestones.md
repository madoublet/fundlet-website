## Overview

By default, the Fundlet leaderboard ranks participants by total sales — or by units, if you've selected that option. Participant milestones build on top of that ranking by awarding medals and prize tiers, so participants have something to chase beyond just the top spot. It makes the leaderboard more fun and gives everyone a reason to push for that first sale.

---

## Step 1: Open the Milestones Settings

1. In the Fundlet app, open your fundraiser.
2. Click **Manage Participants**.
3. From the **More actions** menu, select **Milestones**.

---

## Step 2: Choose Your Milestone Types

There are two types of milestones, and you can use either one or both.

**Medals** award first, second, and third place based on leaderboard position. A participant has to have at least one sale to earn a medal — no sales, no medal.

**Tiers** are custom prize levels you define yourself, based on sales amounts. For example, you might set up three prize levels at $100, $250, and $500.

Hit **Save** when you're done. Milestones are applied immediately — you'll see them reflected in the participant list right away, with top sellers already holding their first-place and prize milestones.

---

## Step 3: See Milestones on the Storefront

Refresh your fundraiser page and the milestones appear on the leaderboard:

- **Earned medals** show next to each participant where a milestone has been reached. A participant who is the top seller and has crossed the first prize tier will show two medals — one for first place, one for prize number one.
- **A progress bar** shows how close each participant is to their next milestone, and what it will take to reach prize number two and prize number three.

This is a great way to incentivize those initial sales and keep the leaderboard interesting throughout the campaign.
