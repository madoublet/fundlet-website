## Overview

Email automations let you send emails based on events that happen in your campaign — no manual scheduling and no missed moments. Define your communication strategy once, and it applies automatically to every fundraiser you run across your shop.

Email automations are a **Pro feature**.

---

## Requirement: Start and End Dates

Automations key off of your campaign's schedule, so each campaign must have a **start date** and an **end date**. These dates are what the timing options are measured against.

---

## Step 1: Open Email Automations

If you are signed up for Pro, click **Email Automations** in the Fundlet app to start customizing your automations.

---

## Step 2: Add an Automation

Click **Add Automation** and choose the timing. Available timing options include:

- **One day after start**
- **Start day**
- **Midway through the campaign**
- **Two days before the campaign ends**
- **Campaign last day**

Each automation you add fires at the point you select, relative to the campaign's start and end dates.

---

## Step 3: Choose Who Receives It

Select the audience for the automation. You can send to:

- **Contacts** (campaign organizers)
- **Participants**
- **Both**

---

## Step 4: Write the Subject and Body

Enter the **subject** and **body** for the email. The body supports a wide variety of variables, so you can personalize each message and reuse the same automation across all of your fundraisers.

---

## Step 5: Enable Automations on a Fundraiser

Once your automations are set up, the only step left is to enable them on a campaign. When creating or editing a fundraiser, select **Schedule Email Automations**. From there, the emails kick off automatically based on the campaign's start and end dates.

That's it — set it once, and Fundlet handles the timing for you, keeping your fundraiser top of mind at exactly the right moments.
