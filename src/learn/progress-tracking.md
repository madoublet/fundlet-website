## Overview

Fundlet's progress section used to track one thing: the payout to the organization. Now you can choose what it measures — payout, total revenue, or units sold — so the progress bar reflects whatever goal your fundraiser is actually chasing.

As part of this change, the campaign **goal** has moved out of the payout section and into the new **Progress** section, where it sits alongside the metric you're tracking.

---

## Step 1: Open the Progress Section

1. In the Fundlet app, open your fundraiser and click **Edit Campaign**.
2. Scroll down to the **Progress** section.

---

## Step 2: Pick What to Track

Choose one of three options:

- **Payout to the organization** — the original behavior, tracking how much the fundraiser will pay out.
- **Total revenue** — tracks total sales against your goal.
- **Units** — tracks the number of items sold, which works well when your fundraiser has an "amount sold" goal rather than a dollar goal.

Set your goal for the metric you picked (for example, 50 units) and hit **Save**.

---

## Step 3: See It Update Everywhere

The change takes effect immediately:

- **In the app**, the progress bar moves to sit under the block for whatever you're tracking — payout, total sales, or units.
- **On the fundraiser storefront**, refresh the page and the progress bar now measures the metric you selected.
- **At the participant level**, progress is tracked the same way. When you're tracking units, the leaderboard shows units sold for each participant instead of sales totals.
