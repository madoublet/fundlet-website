## Overview

Fundlet lets you customize the colors of any campaign directly — without creating a separate theme template for each organization. You can update the banner, welcome block, product card, and leaderboard to match school colors or team branding in just a few clicks.

---

## Step 1: Open the Campaign and Edit

1. In the Fundlet app, go to **Campaigns** and open the campaign you want to personalize.
2. Click **Edit Campaign** and scroll down to **Design Options**.

---

## Step 2: Customize the Colors

Under **Design Options**, you can select a theme template as your base and then override colors on top of it. For each section — **Banner**, **Welcome Block**, **Product Card**, and **Leaderboard** — you can set:

- **Background color** — pick a color that matches the team or school brand
- **Text color** — choose a contrasting color (e.g. white text on a dark blue background)

---

## Step 3: Save and Preview

Click **Save**. The campaign page will immediately reflect the updated colors. Go back to the campaign and click **View Storefront** to confirm everything looks right.
