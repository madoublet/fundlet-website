## Overview

Fundraiser products often have unique pricing and shipping needs that differ from your regular catalog. This guide shows how to manage fundraiser products alongside your existing B2C and B2B product lines — keeping their pricing and fulfillment flexible without disrupting your existing setup.

The approach has two parts: create fundraiser products as **unlisted products**, then attach a dedicated **shipping profile** to them.

---

## Step 1: Create Fundraiser Products as Unlisted

Create products specifically for your fundraiser (for example, "Fundraiser Cookies" and "Fundraiser Ice Cream") and set their status to **Unlisted**.

The advantage is that unlisted products live alongside your existing products but won't show up in your storefront search, your collections, or Google search. They can still have their own stock and inventory, their own pricing, and their own shipping profiles — giving each fundraiser product a clean separation from your regular B2C and B2B lines.

For a deeper walkthrough of creating unlisted products, see [Use Unlisted Shopify Products in a Fundraiser](/learn/unlisted-shopify-products).

---

## Step 2: Add the Products to Your Campaign

Because unlisted products don't appear in collections, you add them to your campaign directly:

1. In the Fundlet app, go to **Campaigns** and open your fundraiser.
2. Click **Edit Campaign**.
3. In the **Products** section, add your fundraiser products (for example, Fundraiser Cookies and Fundraiser Ice Cream).
4. Save.

Those products are now the items sold on your campaign page, kept separate from the rest of your store.

---

## Step 3: Create a Fundraiser Shipping Profile

Shipping is handled within Shopify using **shipping profiles**. In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings** and search for **shipping profiles**.

Create a shipping profile dedicated to your fundraisers. Depending on how your fundraisers run, you may want more than one — for example:

- **Fundraiser Ship** — for fundraisers that ship products to supporters
- **Fundraiser No Ship** — for fundraisers that only do local pickup and delivery

Keeping these as separate profiles lets you run fundraisers with different fulfillment models side by side.

---

## Step 4: Configure Shipping Options

Attach the shipping profile to your fundraiser products and set up the shipping options you want. For example, on a "Fundraiser Ship" profile attached to your fundraiser cookies, you might set up:

- A **flat rate** shipping option (for example, $10) for shipping directly to the supporter
- A **free** option for fundraiser pickup — where products are dropped off with the campaign organizer to distribute

At checkout, supporters then see the choice: pay for shipping to have it sent directly to them, or use the free option to have the fundraiser deliver it.

---

## Wrap-up

With unlisted products plus dedicated shipping profiles, you can separate your fundraiser products from your existing catalog and handle unique pricing and shipping constraints — all within the Fundlet and Shopify ecosystem, without affecting your regular product lineup.
