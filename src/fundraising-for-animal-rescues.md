**Short answer:** An animal shelter or rescue can run a product fundraiser by partnering with a shop that uses Fundlet. The shop sells its products through a branded campaign page in the rescue's name, volunteers and fosters share links, and the agreed share of proceeds funds the rescue. Every order is recorded, with no cash handling and no event to staff.

Rescue finances are relentless and unpredictable. A single emergency surgery can consume a month's donations, foster supplies are a continuous drain, and the organization is run by volunteers who are already driving transports on weekends and answering adoption emails at midnight.

---

## No event to staff

Adoption events, bake sales, and tabling all cost volunteer hours the organization does not have. A link-based campaign costs essentially none: share the link on the pages you already post to, and the dashboard tracks itself while your volunteers do the actual work of animal care.

---

## Fosters, volunteers, and adopters all count

Rescues have an unusually large and emotionally invested network. Every foster, volunteer, and past adopter can be a participant with their own link and total — and adopters in particular are a nearly untouched audience who are glad to be asked.

- A link per foster, volunteer, or adopter
- Live per-participant and per-campaign totals
- Group participants by foster team or region
- Exportable records for your treasurer

---

## Emergency vet bills and short windows

When a dog needs a four-thousand-dollar surgery this week, a grant cycle is useless. A campaign can be opened and closed in days, tied to a specific animal's name and need — which is the kind of concrete, urgent story that converts far better than a general appeal.

---

## Named campaigns for specific animals

Rescue supporters respond to individuals, not categories. Running a campaign per animal or per medical case puts a name and a goal on the page, and a visible progress bar gives the audience a reason to come back and to share it further.

---

## Beyond pet products

A rescue is not limited to selling pet supplies. A bakery, coffee roaster, or candle maker works just as well, and often better, because the audience is buying for themselves — the connection to the cause is the campaign, not the product category.

---

## Restricted medical funds, kept separate

Donations given for a specific animal are restricted, and mixing them is a real governance problem. One campaign per case keeps the proceeds separated by construction, with a transaction record you can export for your books.
