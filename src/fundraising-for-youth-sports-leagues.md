**Short answer:** A youth sports league can run a single fundraising program across all of its teams using Fundlet. Each team gets its own campaign page, goal, and roster, while the league keeps visibility across all of them. Orders are credited to individual players automatically, so no volunteer coach has to collect cash or tally an order form.

A league is not one organization — it is twenty or fifty small ones, each run by a parent volunteer with a day job. Anything that requires every coach to be organized will fail somewhere. The point of a league-wide program is that the structure holds even when the volunteers change.

---

## One program, many teams

Fundlet supports unlimited simultaneous campaigns, which is what makes a league workable. The U10 girls and the U14 boys each get a separate page, separate goal, and separate roster, but they are all running the same program from the same shop with the same products.

- Each team's progress is tracked on its own
- Teams can start and end on different dates
- A coach only ever sees what their team needs to do
- The league is not blocked waiting on the slowest team

---

## Friendly competition between teams

Because every team has its own live total, a league-wide drive produces a leaderboard for free. Leagues use this deliberately: a prize for the top-selling team, recognition at opening day, or a split where a share of each team's sales funds league-wide costs like field maintenance and umpires.

---

## Registration-fee relief

Many leagues would rather not raise fees. A product drive is an alternative: sales credited to a specific player can offset that player's registration or travel costs, which lets families who cannot write a check at signup participate by selling instead. Per-player totals are exactly what that policy needs to be fair and auditable.

---

## When volunteers turn over

Every league loses coaches and board members each year. Because campaign setup is a single onboarding form and the tracking is automatic, handing a team's fundraiser to a new parent takes minutes, and last season's records do not leave with the person who kept them.

---

## No cash in circulation

Youth leagues have a particular reason to care that no money moves through children's hands. Supporters pay the shop directly at a standard online checkout. There are no envelopes, no reimbursement requests, and no order forms carrying neighbors' addresses in a backpack.
